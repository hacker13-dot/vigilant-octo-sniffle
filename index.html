Our Story

Born from a Terrible Sandwich
and an Extraordinary Cheese

TheBigCheese.com didn't start in a boardroom or a business school. It started in a tiny apartment kitchen, at 2am, with a block of the most life-changing cheese its founder had ever tasted — and an absolute fury that nobody else could find it.

🧀
Est. 2021
2019

The Sandwich That Changed Everything

Our founder, Jamie, was a regular person living a regular life — eating regular cheese. Shrink-wrapped, orange, forgettable. Then a friend came back from a trip to a tiny village in the French Alps and brought a wedge of 18-month mountain Comté wrapped in wax paper and newspaper. Jamie ate it standing over the kitchen counter at midnight.

"I didn't know cheese could do that. I sat down on the floor. I genuinely had to sit down."

The next day, Jamie tried to buy more. There was nowhere. Not locally. Not online — not really. There were a few specialist shops, a handful of mail-order services, but nothing that felt like it was built for people who actually cared. No community. No discovery. No conversation between the farmer who made the cheese and the person who needed it.

2020

The Lockdown Revelation

When the world locked down, something unexpected happened in kitchens everywhere. People — suddenly home, suddenly cooking, suddenly paying attention — rediscovered food. Sourdough bread. Homemade pasta. And cheese. Real cheese.

Jamie spent six months calling artisan cheesemakers across Europe and the USA. Farmhouse producers in Somerset. Alpine dairies in Fribourg. Cave-agers in the Aveyron. Family operations in Puglia and Cork and Asturias. Most of them had incredible, world-class cheese sitting in their cellars — and almost no way to reach the people who would love it most.

"One cheesemaker in County Cork told me he'd been making his cheese for 40 years and his biggest customer was a deli in Dublin that sold 3 wheels a month. His cheese was extraordinary. It deserved the world."

That phone call sealed it. TheBigCheese.com had to exist.

2021

We Launched with 12 Cheeses and Zero Sleep

The first version of TheBigCheese.com launched on a Tuesday morning in March 2021. We had 12 cheeses, a website built on determination, a cold-chain logistics partnership we'd spent four months negotiating, and approximately zero marketing budget.

We sold out in 48 hours. Not because we were famous. Because there were people — thousands of them — who had been waiting for exactly this without knowing it. People who had eaten something extraordinary once and never found it again. People who had made cheese at home and wanted to trade. People who had visited a farmers' market and thought: why can't I get this delivered?

"The first review we ever got said: 'I cried a little. That's embarrassing to admit but it's the truth. It tasted like the holiday I took 10 years ago and thought I'd never get back.' That's why we do this."

Today

162 Cheeses. 430 Makers. One Mission.

Today TheBigCheese.com is home to over 162 cheeses from 40 countries, 430 artisan sellers, and a community of 700,000 cheese lovers who trade, review, discover and obsess together. We have a monthly cheese club. We run raffles that give people the chance to win a five-pound wheel of Parmigiano. We have a cheese board builder that lets you plan the perfect spread for any occasion.

But the mission is exactly the same as it was at 2am over that block of Comté.

"Great cheese should not be a secret. It should not live only in specialist shops in major cities or on the tables of people who already know. It belongs to everyone who is willing to be curious."

We believe a piece of cheese can transport you. It can taste like a particular valley, a specific altitude, a particular season, a farmer who has been doing the same thing for 40 years because they love it. We believe that deserves to be celebrated — and shared.

TheBigCheese.com exists so that the next person who tastes something extraordinary at 2am never has to go without it again.
